Why Lower Valley Homes Need a Roof Inspection Now

Lower Valley El Paso has a large concentration of homes built between the 1960s and 1990s. Many of those original roofing systems have not been replaced. Even if there are no visible leaks inside, these older roofs may be:

  • Carrying two or more layers of shingles, which holds extra weight and traps heat in the attic
  • Built with flat or low-slope sections that have cracked, split, or delaminated over time
  • Missing proper flashing around swamp cooler pads, which is one of the most common leak sources we find in Lower Valley homes
  • Suffering from deteriorated roof-to-wall connections on room additions and casitas
  • Lacking drainage slope, which leads to ponding after heavy rains and accelerates deterioration

A free inspection from Star City Roofing takes about 30 minutes and gives you a clear picture of where your roof actually stands, with no pressure to do anything.

Lower Valley Roofing, FAQ

How much does roof replacement cost in Lower Valley El Paso? +
For a typical Lower Valley home in the 1,200 to 2,000 square foot range, a full roof replacement generally runs $8,000 to $14,000. Homes with flat roof sections, room additions, or significant decking damage will add to the cost. We give you a fully itemized written estimate before any work starts, no surprises.
My Lower Valley home has a flat roof section. What are my options? +
Flat and low-slope roofs are very common on older homes in Ysleta and the surrounding Lower Valley neighborhoods. If the base structure is still sound, an elastomeric or silicone roof coating is often the best option. It seals the surface, stops leaks, and dramatically reduces heat gain inside the house. If the existing roofing material is too far gone, we can install a new flat roof system first and then apply the coating on top. We will tell you honestly which situation you are in after the inspection.
Does flooding in the Lower Valley affect my roof? +
It can. Lower Valley El Paso sits at a lower elevation than much of the city, and during heavy summer storms the area sees some of the most intense rainfall in the metro. Flat roofs are especially vulnerable to ponding water when drains or scuppers are blocked. Even small failures in flashing or seams can let water in fast when rain is sitting on the surface. After any major storm, a quick inspection is worth it. We offer free post-storm inspections for Lower Valley homeowners.
How do I know if I need a full roof replacement or just a repair? +
This is what the free inspection is for. If your roof is under 15 years old and the damage is localized, a repair usually makes sense. If it is 20-plus years old, has been patched multiple times, or has widespread wear across the shingles or flat sections, replacement is usually the better investment long term. We will give you a straight answer on which one fits your situation, and we do not push replacements when a repair will do the job.
Do you help with insurance claims for storm damage in the Lower Valley? +
Yes. We document the damage with photos and measurements, work with your adjuster during the inspection, and make sure everything is properly recorded so your claim is processed correctly. We cannot guarantee claim outcomes, but we have helped many Lower Valley homeowners navigate the process and get their roofs covered. Call us right after the storm and we will get out there fast.
